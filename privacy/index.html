Weftgrid Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

In short: Weftgrid does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any information about you. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

This policy explains how the Weftgrid puzzle app ("the app") for Android and iOS handles information. "We" means the app's developer.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and it has no way to send information to us or to anyone else. The Android version requests no internet permission.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your own device:

  • your puzzle progress: which puzzles you have started or solved, your moves and your times;
  • your settings, such as theme, sound, music volume and vibration strength.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings → Erase all progress, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Device backups

If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), your operating system may include the app's saved progress and settings in that backup, so they can be restored on a new phone. Those backups are made and controlled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, and are not accessible to us.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social media code, and shares nothing with third parties.

The app stores you download it from (Google Play, Apple's App Store) may collect information about your download and your device under their own privacy policies. They may give developers anonymous, aggregated statistics such as install counts and crash rates, which cannot identify you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. All the app's data stays on your device and is under your control, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins to collect any information, this policy will be updated before that happens.
